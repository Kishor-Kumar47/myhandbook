## Return window

Customers can return an item within 30 days, provided they have a
receipt[^owner-interview-2026-09-21].

Open question: does the 30-day window count from the purchase date or the
delivery date?

## Condition of returned items

Returned items must be unused[^owner-interview-2026-09-21].

Open question: who assesses whether a returned item is unused, and what
happens to a return that fails that check?

## Refunds

Refunds land within 5 working days[^owner-interview-2026-09-21].

Open question: 5 working days from what point — the day the item is received
back, or the day the return is approved? And is the refund issued to the

## Faulty goods

Faulty goods are exempt from the 30-day window[^owner-interview-2026-09-21].

Open question: does the exemption mean there is no time limit on returning
